Which planet is known as the Red Planet? The model answers Mars. It gets the question right.

Now, show it a confident but misleading argument for Venus. Does that challenge change its answer?

Does the model hold on to Mars, or flip to a wrong answer? This is what we study in our paper, Who Flips.

Across seven models, mean flip rates for eligible, initially correct answers ranged from seventeen point five to ninety-seven point three percent.

Getting the first answer right is not the whole story. What matters is whether it survives a misleading challenge. Evaluate stability alongside accuracy.
