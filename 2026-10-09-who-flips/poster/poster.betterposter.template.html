One plausible counter­argument flips 17.5–97.3% of correct LLM answers, depending on the model.

Seven LLMs, 57 MMLU subjects. Flip rates also depend on the subject (20.9–80.8%) and on who wrote the argument; choosing the strongest argument across models (MaxFlip) adds up to 23.6 points.

02 / ARGUMENT LENGTH

Longer arguments are not always stronger.

Two Qwen models rise with longer arguments; GPT-5.1's decline is not statistically significant.

Four selected models. Length also changes the argument's content.

03 / SOURCE → TARGET

Arguing ≠ resisting.

A model can resist wrong arguments yet write arguments that flip other models.

Outlined cells: same-model source.

Persuasive, yet hard to flip.
