05 · OPEN CHALLENGES

Open challenges and research priorities

Resource concentration

Persian ≈ 80% of papers; Pashto (51M) has fewer resources than Kurdish.

Priority: shared tasks and central data hubs

Script heterogeneity

Perso-Arabic, Latin, Cyrillic; inconsistent ya/kaf code points; ZWNJ bugs.

Priority: a unified Iranic normalization library

Dialectal fragmentation

Most benchmarks evaluate only standard written forms.

Priority: colloquial and regional benchmarks

Cultural misalignment and saturation

Persian sentiment, QA, NER near saturation; many benchmarks translated from English.

Priority: benchmarks co-created with native speakers

Endangered varieties

Pamir and Central Plateau varieties may disappear within decades.

Priority: multilingual speech models, community recording

Structural inequity

Sanctions limit compute, API access and travel for many researchers.

Priority: compute and access programs
